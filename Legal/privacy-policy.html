Privacy Policy

AiroSync Ecosystem (Desktop & Mobile)
Effective Date: October 7, 2026 | Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy: We do not collect, monetize, track, or store your personal files on cloud servers. All communications, transfers, and controls operate purely peer-to-peer over your private Local Area Network (Wi-Fi/LAN).

1. Overview & Data Architecture

AiroSync is a cross-platform utility designed to facilitate direct device-to-device file transfers, clipboard synchronization, and remote device utilities across mobile and desktop devices connected to the same Local Area Network (Wi-Fi/LAN).

Because transfers occur directly between the sender and receiver over your local Wi-Fi router or hotspot, no user files or personal data are ever uploaded to external cloud servers, databases, or third parties.

2. What Information We Process

A. Files and Media Content

Any file, image, document, or archive you choose to send is transmitted directly to the paired device on your local network. Transferred files are saved directly to the destination device's designated folder (e.g., Downloads). We do not inspect, log, or maintain records of your transferred content.

B. Clipboard Content

When you explicitly enable Clipboard Synchronization, text copied to your clipboard is transmitted via secure local network sockets directly to your paired device. Clipboard data is not stored remotely or logged.

C. Microphone Audio Streaming (Optional)

If you activate the optional Mic Streaming feature, live audio captured by your mobile device's microphone is encoded and streamed directly to your paired Windows PC via local network socket. Audio is never transmitted to the internet or recorded to any external server.

D. Local Network Discovery Metadata

To detect and pair devices automatically, the application utilizes standard local multicast discovery protocols (mDNS/SSDP). These packets contain device broadcast names and local IP addresses and remain strictly confined within your local Wi-Fi subnet.

3. Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

To provide high-speed local connectivity, the following system permissions are requested on your devices:

Platform Permission / Capability Specific Technical Justification
Android NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES Enables discovering nearby AiroSync PC servers without requesting GPS location (scoped with neverForLocation).
Android ACCESS_COARSE / FINE_LOCATION Scoped strictly to Android 12 and below (API ≤ 32) as required by legacy Android OS for local Wi-Fi scanning. Not used for physical location tracking.
Android CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Allows exchanging mDNS packets on the local router for automatic zero-configuration device pairing.
Android INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required to establish local TCP/WebSocket/HTTP sockets with the paired PC.
Android Storage / Media Permissions Allows users to pick files to send and save incoming files to their local device storage.
Android RECORD_AUDIO Used solely for the optional Microphone Streaming feature over the local network to the paired PC.
Android FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keeps active file transfers running continuously in the background without being killed by OS battery savers.
Windows Local Network / Sockets Binds an HTTP/WebSocket server port on the local network interface for receiving incoming files and commands.
Windows File System Access Reads selected files to transfer and writes incoming files directly to the user's Downloads directory.

4. Third-Party Services, Ads & Analytics

  • No Advertising: AiroSync contains zero advertisements.
  • No Analytics SDKs: No third-party behavioral analytics, trackers, or marketing trackers are bundled in the application.
  • Update Verification: Update checks query public GitHub releases (https://github.com/asepsayyad007/AiroSync-Release) over standard HTTPS to inform you when a new version is available.

5. Security & Encryption

Transfers over the local network utilize TLS/HTTPS and WebSocket Secure (WSS). In addition, connection and pairing requests require user authorization and optional PIN verification to prevent unauthorized transfer attempts on shared Wi-Fi networks.

6. Children's Privacy

AiroSync does not collect personal data from any users, including children under the age of 13. The application is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data remains strictly on your local hardware, there is no remote cloud account or database to delete. You can delete transferred files at any time via your device's file manager, and uninstalling the application removes all application configurations and pairing keys.
